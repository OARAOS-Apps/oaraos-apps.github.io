TinyStep — Privacy Policy

1. The app and contact

TinyStep is a mobile app provided by Erçetin Topal (Oaraos Software). For privacy, support and data requests: oaraos.support@gmail.com

2. Data processed on your device

The habit names and daily check-ins you enter are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone and used to make the app work.

No user account is created. These records are not sent to the developer or any server; we cannot access them remotely.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed only by Google Play or the Apple App Store. To verify and restore purchases, the store processes product and transaction details in its own systems. Card details are handled by the store and never reach us. Records you enter in the app are not sent to the store or any other server.

There are no ads, advertising IDs, cross-app tracking or analytics SDKs.

Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy · Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

4. Permissions

The app asks for a permission only when you use the related feature: POST_NOTIFICATIONS; RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device and may appear on the lock screen depending on your settings. If location or camera is used, images and coordinates are processed on the device. You can revoke permissions at any time in system settings.

5. Backups and files

Backup export/import only uses the file you choose. If you save it to a cloud drive or email, that provider's policy applies. Backup files are not additionally encrypted. Your device's Apple/Google backup features may copy app data according to their own settings.

6. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes local data; exported backups must be deleted separately. Support emails are used only to answer your request and are deleted once it is resolved.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

8. Your rights

You may contact us about access, correction, deletion or objection under the laws that apply to you (e.g. GDPR, Turkish KVKK). You can manage on-device data directly in the app.

9. Changes

If data processing changes, this page and its date will be updated. Version: 11 October 2026.
